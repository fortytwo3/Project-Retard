# Project Retard — Gaming Dropshipping Store

Product research and catalog data for a gaming peripherals, setup gear, and
collectibles dropshipping store.

## What's here

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `research/product-shortlist.md` | **Start here.** 24 vetted products across four tiers with cost, sell price, margin, and the reason each one sells. Includes a 12-SKU launch set and worked unit economics. |
| `research/avoid-list.md` | Eight products that look like winners and aren't, with the specific failure mode for each. |
| `research/compliance-and-risk.md` | IP, counterfeit, payment-processor, and shipping-compliance rules for this niche. |
| `research/suppliers.md` | Supplier comparison by tier, plus a vetting checklist. |
| `data/products.json` | The same catalog as structured data, ready for the storefront page to consume. |

## The short version

- **Peripherals and setup gear are the business.** 65–75% gross margins, light
  shipping, no IP exposure, and every product demos in a five-second video.
- **Start with five SKUs, not forty.** Monitor light strip, RGB deskmat, controller
  dock, mechanical keyboard, VR comfort kit.
- **Figures are the trap.** Unlicensed figures from Chinese marketplaces are the
  fastest way to lose a payment processor. Three safe routes exist — licensed
  distributor wholesale, original-art print-on-demand, and generic non-IP
  collectibles. Print-on-demand is the best of them.
- **AOV beats CAC.** The ad cost is already paid by the time someone's at checkout.
  Order bumps and bundles are what turn a 17% net margin into a real one.
