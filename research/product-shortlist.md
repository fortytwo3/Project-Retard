# Gaming Dropshipping — Vetted Product Shortlist

**Purpose:** a buy-list for a gaming peripherals / setup / collectibles store.
**Last researched:** August 2026.

## How products were selected

Every item below had to clear five filters. Anything that failed two or more is in
`avoid-list.md` instead.

| Filter | Threshold | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ship weight | Under ~2 lb (0.9 kg) | Shipping and returns eat the margin above that |
| Gross margin | 3x cost minimum | 15–30% *net* survives after ads, fees, returns |
| IP exposure | No third-party trademarks | Trademark claims kill stores and freeze payouts |
| Demoability | Visible in a 5-second video | Paid social is the acquisition channel |
| Return rate | Low failure / low sizing risk | Electronics with batteries and moving parts return more |

**On the price numbers:** cost figures are typical landed-unit ranges for
AliExpress / CJ-tier suppliers, and sell prices are what comparable listings hold on
Shopify stores and Amazon. They are planning estimates, not live quotes — pull real
quotes from two suppliers before you commit to a price on any SKU.

## Tier C — Anchor / high-ticket ($100+)

Two of these are here to anchor your pricing page, not to be your volume. Read the
warnings.

| # | Product | Cost | Sell | Gross | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Sim-racing wheel stand (foldable, wheel+pedal mount) | $60–95 | $189–279 | ~62% | **Freight-sensitive.** Only viable through a US/EU warehouse supplier. A single return can wipe out three sales. |
| C2 | Ergonomic gaming chair | $70–120 | $229–349 | ~63% | **Do not launch with this.** Heavy freight, high damage-in-transit rate, expensive returns. Add it in month 4+ and only from a domestic warehouse. |
| C3 | RGB gaming desk with carbon-fiber top + cable tray | $85–140 | $259–399 | ~62% | Same freight warning as C2. Good margin, bad first product. |
| C4 | Curated "full setup" bundle (B1 + B2 + B3 + A2) | $70–105 | $249–329 | ~62% | **Build this instead of C1–C3.** All-light shipping, no freight risk, and it triples AOV using inventory you already carry. |

The honest read on Tier C: **C4 is the one to build.** C1–C3 look attractive on a
margin spreadsheet and lose money in practice on freight and returns until you have
volume and a domestic 3PL.

## Tier D — Figures & collectibles

You asked for figures specifically. This category has the best emotional pull in
gaming and by far the worst legal profile. There are three routes; two are safe.

### Route 1 — Licensed distributor wholesale (safe, thinner margin)

Buy authentic product through authorized distributors. Margins drop to 25–40% and
you may need a resale certificate and a minimum order, but you can advertise brand
names legally and your store won't get pulled.

- **Bandai / Banpresto / Tamashii Nations** — via authorized US distributors (Bluefin
  Distribution is the main one)
- **Good Smile Company** (Nendoroid, figma) — distributor and preorder programs
- **Kotobukiya** — model kits and statues
- **Great Eastern Entertainment / Bioworld** — licensed apparel and small goods

This is the only route where you can put a franchise name in your product title.

### Route 2 — Print-on-demand original art (safest, best margin)

You commission the art, so you own it. Zero IP risk, no inventory, 40–60% margin.

- Original-art posters and canvas prints ($6–12 → $29–49)
- Original-art mousepads and deskmats ($9–14 → $32–49)
- Acrylic standees and desk figures from your own designs ($4–8 → $19–34)
- Gamer apparel with original slogans ($9–15 → $32–45)

Suppliers: Printful, Printify, Gelato. Commission a freelance illustrator for a
6–10 piece original series. **This is the highest-leverage move in the whole
category** — it's the only product on this list a competitor can't copy by finding
your supplier.

### Route 3 — Generic non-IP collectibles (safe, medium margin)

Physical collectibles that don't reference any franchise:

- Articulated blank/artist desk figures ($5–10 → $24–39)
- Non-licensed mecha model kits ($12–22 → $44–69)
- Metal/resin dice sets in display cases ($6–12 → $26–45) — TTRPG crossover, strong repeat rate
- Display shelves, acrylic cases, and figure risers ($8–16 → $34–59) — sells *to* collectors without selling *them* a licensed figure

### Route 4 — Unlicensed figures from Chinese marketplaces — **do not**

Anime and game figures on AliExpress/CJ are overwhelmingly bootlegs. Selling them
risks Shopify store termination, Stripe/PayPal account freezes with held funds, and
direct liability — and your Chinese supplier will not indemnify you. Rights holders
(Bandai, Toei, Shueisha, Nintendo, Pokémon Company) actively issue takedowns, and
they target the images and franchise names in your listings and ads as well as the
product itself. Skip it. See `compliance-and-risk.md`.

## Suggested launch set (12 SKUs)

Don't launch 40 products. Launch this and let data cut it down.

**Core (test with ads):** A1 monitor light strip, A2 RGB deskmat, A3 controller dock,
B1 mechanical keyboard, B8 VR comfort kit

**Attach / order-bumps:** A6 thumbstick grips, A9 mouse skates, A5 keycap set,
A7 cable kit

**AOV builders:** A10 monitor light bar, B5 streaming kit, C4 full-setup bundle

**Brand layer:** one 6-piece original-art print-on-demand series (Route 2) — no
inventory risk, and it's what makes the store look like a brand instead of a
reseller.

## Unit economics — the number that actually matters

Gross margin is not profit. Worked example on A2 (RGB deskmat) at a $39 sell price:

```
Sell price                    $39.00
Product cost                 -$10.00
Shipping (supplier)           -$4.00
Payment processing (~3%)      -$1.20
                             --------
Contribution before ads       $23.80
Ad spend at 2.5x ROAS        -$15.60
Returns/replacements (~4%)    -$1.56
                             --------
Net per order                  $6.64   (17% net margin)
```

Breakeven ROAS at these numbers is about **1.6x**. Anything above that is profit;
anything below and you're buying revenue with your own money. Track this per SKU
from day one — it's the only metric that tells you which products to kill.

The lever that changes this math isn't a better ad. It's AOV: attach A6 ($15) and
A5 ($34) to that same order and net per order roughly triples, because the ad cost
is already paid.
