# Suppliers — who to source each tier from

## Quick comparison

| Supplier | Best for | Typical US delivery | Catalog | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Spocket** | Fastest US/EU delivery | 3–6 days | Smaller, curated | Highest-quality shipping experience; narrower gaming selection |
| **Zendrop** | US-focused beginners | 5–8 days | Medium | Good branding/custom-packaging support, automated fulfillment |
| **CJ Dropshipping** | Widest gaming catalog | 8–20 days (7–12 from US warehouse) | Very large, 50+ warehouses | Best sourcing depth, factory customization, private inventory. Use their US warehouse stock where available |
| **AutoDS** | Product research + automation | Varies by source | Aggregator | Strongest for finding and monitoring winners across sources |
| **AliExpress direct** | Cheapest testing | 12–25 days | Effectively unlimited | Fine for validating demand, too slow to scale on |
| **Printful / Printify / Gelato** | Print-on-demand originals | 4–8 days | POD catalog | Zero IP risk, your own art, no inventory |
| **Bluefin Distribution** | Licensed Bandai/Tamashii figures | Domestic | Licensed only | Authorized US distributor; requires wholesale account + resale cert |
| **Good Smile / Kotobukiya distribution** | Licensed statues & kits | Domestic | Licensed only | Preorder-driven; long lead times, thin margins, fully legal |

## Recommended sourcing plan by tier

**Tier A (impulse):** Start on AliExpress or CJ to validate demand cheaply. The
moment a SKU proves out, move it to CJ's US warehouse or Spocket — delivery time is
the biggest driver of chargebacks in this niche.

**Tier B (margin drivers):** CJ with US warehouse stock, or Zendrop. Order a sample
of every Tier B SKU yourself before listing it. B3 (headsets) and B6 (macro pads)
especially — you're checking mic quality and configuration software, which no
supplier photo will tell you.

**Tier C (anchor):** Domestic warehouse only. Never air-freight furniture from Asia.
Or skip C1–C3 entirely and build the C4 bundle from Tier A/B stock instead.

**Tier D (collectibles):**
- Route 1 (licensed): Bluefin, Good Smile, Kotobukiya distribution — real wholesale
  accounts, resale certificate, minimum orders
- Route 2 (original art POD): Printful or Printify — the highest-leverage option
- Route 3 (generic collectibles): CJ or AliExpress is fine, since there's no IP to
  infringe

## Vetting checklist for any new supplier

1. Order a sample to your own address — always, no exceptions
2. Ask for the FCC/CE certificate on anything electronic and keep the file
3. Ask for the watt-hour rating on anything with a battery
4. Check their processing time separately from shipping time (a "7-day shipping"
   supplier with 5-day processing is a 12-day supplier)
5. Confirm what happens on a defective unit — who pays return freight, and do they
   reship free
6. Confirm they'll ship in unbranded packaging with no supplier invoice inside
7. Ask for their stock level and restock cadence on your hero SKU — a winner that
   goes out of stock mid-campaign burns your ad spend
8. Have a second supplier identified for every hero SKU before you scale it
